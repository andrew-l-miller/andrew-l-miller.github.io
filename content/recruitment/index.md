## Join Gravatars

I welcome inquiries from prospective Master's and PhD students, visiting researchers and postdoctoral fellows interested in gravitational-wave physics, dark matter, compact objects and gravitational-wave data analysis at ICTP-AP, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS), Beijing.

Learn more about our research and team on the [Gravatars website](https://the-gravatars.github.io/).

## Master's and PhD programmes — 2027 admission

The [UCAS 2027 call for international Master's and doctoral students](https://english.ucas.ac.cn/index.php/admission/international-students/notice/6821-call-for-2027-master-s-doctoral-degree-programs-for-international-students) provides information on admission requirements and applications.

Potential funding routes include:

- **UCAS Scholarship for International Students:** full or partial scholarship support, subject to eligibility and selection.
- **CAS-ANSO Scholarship:** graduate scholarship opportunities for eligible international students.
- **Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC):** funding for eligible international graduate students, subject to application and nomination requirements.

Consult the official call for current deadlines, eligibility and application procedures. Scholarship availability and supervisor support are not guaranteed.

## Visiting students

The CAS International Visiting Student Program provides opportunities for short-term research visits. Calls typically occur twice per year; dates and funding conditions depend on the individual call.

## Postdoctoral fellows and visiting scientists

The CAS President's International Fellowship Initiative (PIFI) offers funding routes for eligible international researchers. Calls typically occur annually. Please contact me to discuss potential projects and current opportunities.

Contact me at [andrew.miller.ligo@ucas.ac.cn](mailto:andrew.miller.ligo@ucas.ac.cn) with your CV, research interests, relevant experience and intended programme or visit dates. Prospective graduate students may also contact [Huaike Guo](mailto:guohuaike@ucas.ac.cn).
