Stochastic gravitational-wave backgrounds can encode information about unresolved astrophysical populations and early-Universe physics. My research interests include using background searches to constrain neutron star populations and investigating prospects for probing fundamental physics and cosmological phase transitions.

**Selected publications**

- Detection of early-universe gravitational-wave signatures and fundamental physics — [Journal](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10714-022-03027-x) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.07972)
- Probing ensemble properties of vortex-avalanche pulsar glitches with a stochastic gravitational-wave background search — [Journal](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.107.102001) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.16857)
- Stochastic gravitational-wave background searches and constraints on neutron-star ellipticity · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.03536)
- Search for anisotropic gravitational-wave backgrounds using data from Advanced LIGO and Advanced Virgo's first three observing runs — [Journal](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.104.022005) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.08520)
